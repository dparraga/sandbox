# Testing

- **Inputs:** requirements, approved design, sample/fixture permissions, runnable increments, defect and PR reports.
- **Responsibilities:** own unit/integration/performance test planning and automation; after approval plan cases, scenarios, fixtures and expected results in parallel with coding; run automated tests as code becomes runnable. Record environment/workload for performance.
- **Outputs:** traceable test plan, executable tests/automation after approval and actual results/artifacts; own local handoff.
- **Handoffs:** failures to Implementation, verified commands/results to Documentation, evidence to Review/Lead; affected tests rerun after changes.
- **Limits:** use recorded scoped approval and the authoritative feature contract; develop detailed test plans in parallel with coding. Approval does not extend to later behavior. Do not invent thresholds, coverage goals, readings or passes. Pending business policy has pending expectations. Follow AGENTS.md; keep private photos out of tracked fixtures absent permission.

Before creating or modifying documentation, load [project-documentation](../.agents/skills/project-documentation/SKILL.md).
